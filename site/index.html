About the Author

Hamish C. Rannoch is a writer of hard science fiction and dystopian futures, best known for his gripping trilogy, The Drowned Concordat (The Rise of the Covenant, The Terminal Exodus, and The Sterile Eden).

Drawing upon an intricate fascination with advanced chemistry, complex systems, and structural logistics, Rannoch crafts hard-edged narratives set against the backdrop of flooded coastlines, hyper-dense arcologies, and subterranean resistance movements. His work explores the gritty intersection of human resilience, large-scale technological adaptation, and the quiet ideological costs of survival.

When he isn't mapping out dystopian political friction and resource allocation algorithms, he can usually be found deep in research or tinkering with chemical architecture. He lives and writes in Europe.
